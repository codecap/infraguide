  When you visit this site, GitHub processes technical data (such as IP address,

  operating this website).
  </p>
  <h2>3. Server logs</h2>
  <p>
  GitHub, as our hosting provider, automatically collects standard server log
  data, including IP address, requested page, referrer, browser/device
  information, and timestamp. These logs are used solely for security and
  operational purposes (e.g. detecting abuse) and are not used for tracking or
  analytics. GitHub does not publish a fixed retention period for this data; it
  is retained only as long as necessary for these purposes, per GitHub's
  General Privacy Statement.

  Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).
  </p>
  <h2>4. Cookie consent management (Cookiebot)</h2>
  <p>
  This website uses Cookiebot, a cookie consent management platform provided by
  Cybot A/S, Havnegade 39, 1058 Copenhagen, Denmark, to obtain and record your
  consent before any non-essential cookies (such as Google Analytics) are
  activated.

  To do so, Cookiebot processes a partially anonymized IP address (last digits
  removed), your consent status, the timestamp of your consent, and your
  browser information. This data is stored within the EU and is not transferred
  to third countries.

  Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation to document consent) in
  conjunction with Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in demonstrable,
  transparent consent management).

  Data retention: consent records are stored for 12 months, after which you will
  be asked for consent again.

  For details, see Cybot's privacy policy.
  </p>
  <h2>5. Google Analytics</h2>
  <p>
  This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google
  Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland
  ("Google"). Data may be further processed by Google LLC in the United
  States.

  Google Analytics uses cookies to analyze how visitors use this site (e.g. pages
  visited, session duration, referral source). The data collected may include
  your IP address, device and browser information, and usage behavior. Google
  participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which serves as the legal
  basis for this international data transfer.

  Google Analytics is only activated after you give consent via the cookie
  banner shown on your first visit.

  Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent).

  You can withdraw or change your consent at any time via the cookie settings
  icon at the bottom left of the page.

  Data retention: Event data is retained for 2 months. User data (associated with
  cookies, user identifiers, or advertising identifiers) is retained for 14
  months. The retention period is automatically reset to its full duration
  whenever the user interacts with the site again during this window.
  </p>
  <h2>6. Cookies, summary</h2>

    <li>Request restriction of processing (Art. 18)</li>
    <li>Data portability (Art. 20)</li>
    <li>Object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)</li>
