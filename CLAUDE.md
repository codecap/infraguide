# infraguide

Follow the project's contribution rules, including the commit message format:

@CONTRIBUTING.md

## Privacy policy upkeep

`privacy/index.md` must match what the site actually does. When a change adds
or removes a tool, embed, external resource (scripts, fonts, images from other
hosts), cookie, localStorage key or service provider, update the privacy policy
in the same change and bump its "Last updated" date.
